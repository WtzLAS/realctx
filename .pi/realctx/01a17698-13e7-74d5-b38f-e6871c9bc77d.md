Since the upgrade to @types/node ^26 (with TypeScript ^7), `assert.equal(a, b, msg)` fails typecheck when `msg` is `string | undefined` (e.g. `x.decision?.reasons.join("; ")`). Use `?? ""` for the message. Verify with `npm run typecheck` (tsc --noEmit) and `npm test` (node --test tests/*.test.ts).
