`src/jobs.ts` `pruneOutputsThrottled` runs at most once per DAY across all processes (schedule key `retention` in the shared DB), called at `session_start` and `turn_end` (`extensions/realctx/index.ts`). `pruneOutputs` deletes sessions inactive > `context.retentionDays`, then folders under `<base>/sessions/<project>/<session>` with no `output_sessions` row whose newest mtime is older than the retention period, and `.tmp-` files older than a day in known folders; `retentionDays` 0 deletes nothing. Open sessions call `ContextLayer.touchStore()` at turn end (hourly) so another Pi process's sweep does not prune a long-running session (its `keep` list only has its own open sessions).
