The `skills/realctx/SKILL.md` skill (and the `resources_discover` → `skillPaths` hook in `extensions/realctx/index.ts`) was removed because it mostly duplicated the tool descriptions, `promptGuidelines`, and the frozen `<realctx>` session prompt section (`src/prompt.ts`), and models rarely loaded it. Its unique guidance moved into `extensions/realctx/tools.ts`: path-note semantics → `realctx_recall` description; placeholder variants (`summarized`, `saved as <id>`, `superseded`) and "don't expand just in case / re-read changed files" → `realctx_expand` description; "project-shared is pushed to collaborators, no machine paths" → `realctx_remember` scope param. New agent-facing guidance belongs there, not in a skill.
